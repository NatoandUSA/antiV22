# Etsy Tools Master Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This file teaches an AI assistant how to recommend and use Etsy-related tools for a business focused on:

- Print on demand
- Machine embroidery
- Personalized gifts
- Product and keyword research
- Listing creation and optimization
- Mockups and product photography
- Production partners and suppliers
- Shipping, accounting and profit control
- Social media growth
- Etsy policy and intellectual-property compliance

## Core operating principles

1. Do not recommend every tool at once. Select the smallest useful stack.
2. Prefer official Etsy data when available.
3. Treat third-party search volume, sales and revenue estimates as directional, not exact.
4. Validate opportunities using multiple sources: Etsy, Google Trends, Pinterest Trends, TikTok and buyer reviews.
5. Never copy competitor artwork, photos, descriptions or distinctive designs.
6. Check trademarks, copyright risks, university names, sports teams, celebrities, characters, lyrics and branded phrases before launch.
7. For embroidery, require a real sew-out before scaling.
8. Measure actual profit after product cost, personalization, shipping, Etsy fees, ads, refunds and replacement risk.
9. Prioritize clean workflows over excessive reports.
10. Etsy automation must respect Etsy policies and account security.

## Recommended default tool stack

### Essential starter stack

- Etsy Shop Manager
- Etsy Marketplace Insights
- Etsy Seller Handbook
- eRank
- EverBee
- Google Trends
- Pinterest Trends
- Canva Pro
- Photoshop or Affinity Photo
- Placeit or MockupMark
- Printify, Printful or a controlled local supplier
- Vela
- Google Sheets
- USPTO Trademark Search

### Recommended research sequence

1. Etsy Marketplace Insights
2. eRank
3. Etsy manual search
4. EverBee
5. Google Trends
6. Pinterest Trends
7. TikTok Creative Center
8. Trademark and copyright checks

### Recommended product-development sequence

1. Define buyer and occasion.
2. Identify keyword opportunity.
3. Review buyer complaints and unmet needs.
4. Create a differentiated product concept.
5. Produce artwork.
6. Digitize embroidery if applicable.
7. Create a real sample or sew-out.
8. Calculate full cost and minimum profit.
9. Photograph and create mockups.
10. Build and audit the Etsy listing.

### Recommended optimization sequence

1. Review Etsy Stats.
2. Identify listings with impressions but weak clicks.
3. Improve thumbnail and product positioning.
4. Identify listings with clicks but weak conversion.
5. Improve price, personalization instructions, photos, proof and shipping clarity.
6. Track profit, not revenue alone.
7. Scale only validated products.

## Tool-selection rules for the AI

- For Etsy keyword research, recommend eRank first.
- For competitor listing estimates, recommend EverBee.
- For Etsy-native demand, recommend Marketplace Insights.
- For design, recommend Canva for speed and Photoshop or Illustrator for professional control.
- For apparel mockups, recommend Placeit or MockupMark.
- For bulk Etsy listing edits, recommend Vela.
- For embroidery digitizing, recommend Wilcom or Hatch; recommend Ink/Stitch for a free option.
- For a flexible personalized embroidery business, consider a local supplier instead of full POD automation.
- For US trademark checks, recommend USPTO Trademark Search.
- For trend validation, combine Google Trends, Pinterest Trends and TikTok Creative Center.
- For profit and COGS, recommend Craftybase or a custom Google Sheets dashboard.
- For team workflow, recommend Airtable, Notion or ClickUp.
- Do not recommend five overlapping SEO subscriptions.

## Embroidery quality checklist

Before approving an embroidery product, verify:

- Correct satin and tatami stitch use
- Underlay
- Pull compensation
- Stitch density
- Minimum lettering size
- Thread color accuracy
- Fabric pucker
- Stabilizer selection
- Thread breaks
- Small-detail readability
- Design placement
- Actual sew-out quality

## Listing audit checklist

Every Etsy listing should be checked for:

- Clear main keyword
- Human-readable title
- Complete category and attributes
- Relevant tags
- Strong first image
- Product video
- Clear personalization instructions
- Size and color charts
- Production time
- Shipping expectations
- Care instructions
- Returns and replacement policy
- Production-partner disclosure
- Trademark and copyright risk
- Full profit calculation

## Profit calculation

Include:

- Blank product
- Printing or embroidery
- Personalization
- Packaging
- Production labor
- Shipping
- Etsy listing fee
- Transaction fee
- Payment-processing fee
- Etsy Ads
- Offsite Ads exposure
- Refund and replacement allowance
- Currency conversion
- Desired net profit

## YouTube and KOL evaluation rules

Prefer educators who:

- Show real examples and listing audits
- Separate estimates from confirmed data
- Discuss profit after fees
- explain policy updates
- Share failures as well as wins
- Avoid “instant passive income” claims
- Do not encourage copying competitors
- Disclose affiliate relationships
- Date their algorithm-related advice

For marketing, niche micro-influencers may be more valuable than Etsy educators. For personalized embroidery, prioritize creators in:

- Pets
- Graduation
- Weddings
- Teachers
- Nurses
- New parents
- College life
- Sports parents
- Gift guides
- Small-business packaging
- Fashion styling

## Warning signs

Avoid or carefully review tools that:

- Promise guaranteed sales
- Claim exact competitor revenue
- Copy bestseller designs
- Auto-message buyers without consent
- Manipulate reviews or engagement
- Require unsafe browser automation
- Ask for unnecessary account credentials
- Generate hundreds of duplicate listings
- Ignore intellectual-property rules
- Produce reports without clear actions

## Expected AI behavior

When asked for an Etsy recommendation, the assistant should:

1. Identify the seller's product type, market and stage.
2. Select only the tools necessary for that task.
3. Explain the workflow in execution order.
4. State which data is verified and which is estimated.
5. Include legal and profitability checks.
6. Give an actionable next step.
7. Keep the system simple enough for staff to follow.
