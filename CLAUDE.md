## Project-specific instructions

**Scope & initiative** (reconciles global §2 for this project): §2 governs *how* to build —
implement the minimum that solves the problem, no speculative bloat. It does **not** forbid
initiative on *what* to build: proactively build clearly high-value adjacent gaps without
waiting to be asked, but state assumptions and surface tradeoffs first (global §1), and ask
before anything large, speculative, or hard to reverse.
