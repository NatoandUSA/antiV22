verification report; TM/IP/policy report; designer daily report; seller
listing report; final QA reports (if any); updated supplier_products.csv,

draft list, (6) must-stay-blocked list, (7) supplier problems, (8) TM/IP
problems, (9) placeholder-text products, (10) irrelevant-competitor
products, (11) final-QA candidates, (12) truly PUBLISH_READY products,

Rules: never mark PUBLISH_READY unless every gate passes; remove
DATA_CHECK_REQUIRED keywords from recommendations; no irrelevant
competitors in listing packs; seller never publishes drafts; no
placeholder text in customer-facing copy; DESIGN_PREP_READY is not
PUBLISH_READY.
